# Do not upload list

The following should not be added to this public repository:

- raw third-party patient-level clinical trial source tables;
- raw expression matrices or raw RNA-seq source files;
- cBioPortal/DataHub source files such as `data_mrna_seq_tpm.txt`, `data_clinical_patient.txt`, or `data_clinical_sample.txt`;
- raw JAVELIN supplementary workbooks or extracted patient-level clinical tables;
- raw CheckMate/Braun clinical or expression source workbooks;
- controlled-access data;
- files containing local user paths, private logs, credentials, tokens, email-password data, or identifiable private information;
- cover letters or reviewer-suggestion files;
- obsolete manuscript drafts not clearly marked as archival.
